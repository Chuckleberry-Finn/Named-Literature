description=[h3][b]This mod does not use item scripts. Everything is lua based.[/b][/h3]
description=Applied to: Books, Paperbacks (including every genre/subject book type), Magazines, HottieZs, Comic-Books.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*] Titles are applied randomly to books from a list of over 3000+ titles.
description=[*] Every title is tagged with genres/subjects, so books only appear in matching book types (sci-fi in sci-fi books, classics in classic books, etc.).
description=[*] The 3000+ titles are sorted by year/decade/century written, with a preference for modern (1993); include every century up to the 4th.
description=[*] Book names include the author, and tooltips include the publication date for extra flavor (text).
description=[*] Magazines utilize a similar system but just of magazine titles from a list of 60+ titles, keeping the vanilla issue month/year.
description=
description=[*][B]Additional Features:[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*] Vanilla titles are mixed in alongside the mod's titles, weighted by the size of each title pool. (Can be disabled in sandbox options.)
description=[*] Literature items are color coded (based on a seeded process), colors should always be consistent. Uses vanilla cover variants where available so 3D models match.
description=[/list]
